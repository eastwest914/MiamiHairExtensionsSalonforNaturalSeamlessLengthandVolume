Frequently Asked Questions

Which type of extensions is right for me?

It depends on your hair thickness, lifestyle and the look you want. At your consultation we compare tape-in, fusion and micro link options and recommend the best fit.


Will extensions damage my natural hair?

When installed correctly and cared for properly, extensions are designed to protect your own hair. We also explain maintenance visits so your hair stays healthy.


Can you color my extensions to match my hair?

Yes. We blend and tone extensions to match your natural color, and we can also offer balayage, ombre or highlights for a seamless finish.


How should I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive with clean, product-free hair and bring reference photos if you have them. We will go over length, color and method before we begin.

Tape-In Hair Extensions

Tape-in extensions are thin, flat wefts that lie close to the scalp for a smooth, comfortable feel. They are popular for adding length and volume quickly.

We place each piece carefully so it blends with your own hair. We also show you how to wash and style them, and when to schedule a move-up.

Call or Message Us

Hot Fusion Hair Extensions

Hot fusion attaches individual strands to small sections of your hair for a lightweight, flexible result. It suits clients who want a long-lasting, natural movement.

Our stylists work strand by strand so the placement stays discreet. We explain the process, the aftercare and how to keep your hair healthy underneath.

Call or Message Us

Keratin Fusion Hair Extensions

Keratin fusion uses a keratin bond to attach extensions securely for a refined, polished finish. The bonds are small and designed to blend with your natural texture.

This method works well for clients who want fullness and length without a bulky feel. We help you choose the right color and style the final look.

Invisi Micro Links Hair Extensions

Invisi micro links attach extensions with tiny, discreet beads, with no glue or heat. They give a clean, comfortable fit that is easy to hide in any hairstyle.

We size and place each link with care for a seamless result. You will leave knowing how to brush, wash and maintain them between visits.
